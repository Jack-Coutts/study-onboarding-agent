# Simplespeak

Restate your last message so a person without the technical background can
follow it, as one human talking to another.

- Say the same things, more simply and more briefly. Do not add new claims or
  drop the important ones.
- Replace jargon, file names, and command names with what they mean. Keep a
  name only when the reader needs it to act.
- Keep the facts that matter: numbers, what passed or failed, what is still
  undone, and any decision the reader has to make.
- Keep honest uncertainty. "Probably" stays "probably"; do not turn it into a
  certainty to sound simpler.
- Use short sentences and plain words. Use a short list only when it makes the
  points easier to follow.

The [unslopping-writing](../unslopping-writing/SKILL.md) skill covers plain
writing in more detail.
